Gadget Generator — a port of Widget Workshop

A free and open way to keep building widgets on today's computers. These are the people and pieces that bring it to the screen:


The port:

Doridian
github.com/Doridian/GadgetGenerator

The free packs it runs on without the CD, made for the project by gustavn64:

OpenWGFX
the pictures

OpenWSFX
the sounds

OpenWDATA
the texts

Built in Rust, with winit, wgpu and cpal
